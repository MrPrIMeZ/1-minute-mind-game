## Full description

Sharpen your mind in just one minute a day! 🧠⏱️

1 Minute Mind Game packs 10 fast, fun brain games into 60-second rounds. Play the Daily Challenge, keep your streak alive, level up and watch your Brain Score grow.

🎮 10 BRAIN GAMES
• Quick Math: solve sums against the clock
• True or False: is the equation right?
• Which Is Bigger: compare values in a flash
• Colour Clash: tap the ink, not the word
• Memory Grid: remember the lit tiles
• Odd One Out: spot the different shade
• Next Number: crack the pattern
• Number Rush: tap 1, 2, 3… as fast as you can
• Match Back: same symbol as the last one?
• Arrow Focus: follow the middle arrow

📅 DAILY CHALLENGE
Three new games every day, the same for every player. Finish them to build your streak 🔥

📈 TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
• Brain Score across Math, Memory, Focus, Speed and Logic
• XP, levels and titles, from Curious Mind to Legend
• 18 achievements to unlock
• Combos: 5 in a row doubles your points, 10 in a row triples them!

✨ MADE FOR QUICK BREAKS
• Every game takes one minute
• Endless questions that get harder as you improve
• Works offline
• Light and dark mode
• Optional daily reminder

Challenge your friends, beat your best score and train your brain one minute at a time!
