Privacy Policy

1 Minute Mind Game (Android app, package com.primez.oneminutemind) · Last updated: 28 September 2026

Summary

The game has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location or photos. Your scores, levels and settings are stored only on your phone. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob may collect some device data to show and measure ads, as described below.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (scores, XP, streaks, achievements) and settings (sound, vibration, reminder time) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive this data. It may be included in your own Android device backup if you have backups turned on. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. To do this, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from the IP address. This data is used to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows one daily reminder to play. The reminder is scheduled on your device and no data is sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share any personal data. The only third party that receives data is Google AdMob, as described above. Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit.
