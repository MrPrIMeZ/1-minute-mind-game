Privacy Policy

1 Minute Mind (Android app, package com.primez.oneminutemind) · Last updated: 28 September 2026

Summary

The game never asks for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, location or photos. Your levels and settings are stored on your phone. If you choose to join the online scoreboard, we store the username and state you pick and your daily challenge scores. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect some device data to show and measure ads. Premium is bought through Google Play, and we never see your payment details.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (scores, XP, streaks, achievements) and settings (sound, vibration, reminder time) are saved in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive this data. It may be included in your own Android device backup if you have backups turned on. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Online scoreboard (optional)

Joining the scoreboard is optional. If you sign up, we store on our database (Supabase, hosted in India):

  • the username and state you choose (shown publicly on the scoreboard),
  • your daily challenge totals and the dates they were played (shown publicly as rankings),
  • whether you have Premium (shown as a crown next to your name),
  • a random player ID and secret code created by the app, so only your phone can change your scores.

We use this only to run the scoreboard. It is not linked to your advertising ID or any other identity.

Deleting your scoreboard profile

In the app: open Scoreboard → Edit → Delete my scoreboard profile. Your username, state and all your scores are deleted from our database straight away.

Without the app: email primezassociates@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my 1 Minute Mind scoreboard profile" and your username. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm. Game progress on your phone is removed by Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling.

Purchases (Premium)

Premium is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under the Google Payments privacy notice; we only receive confirmation that Premium was bought, never your card or bank details.

Sharing your Brain Score

When you tap Share, the app draws a picture on your phone and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). Nothing is uploaded to us. What happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. To do this, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system, app interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from the IP address. This data is used to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows one daily reminder to play. The reminder is scheduled on your device and no data is sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings.

Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal data. Third parties that process data are Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (purchases) and Supabase (scoreboard database), only as described above. All data is encrypted in transit.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions? Contact us at primezassociates@gmail.com.
